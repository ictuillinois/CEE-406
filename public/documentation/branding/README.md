# Software artwork

These assets identify the software discussed in the course. Original colors and
proportions are retained. Marks belong to their respective owners; their display
does not imply endorsement. Source check: October 1, 2026.

- **FAARFIELD:** `faarfield-icon.png` and `faarfield-logo.png` are PNG encodings of
  `FF2/Resources/FAA-Icon.ico` in the supplied FAARFIELD 2.1.1 source. The project
  explicitly selects that resource as `ApplicationIcon`. It is the FAA seal used
  by the application, not a newly drawn FAARFIELD logo.
  [FAA software page](https://www.airporttech.tc.faa.gov/Products/Airport-Safety-Papers-Publications/Airport-Safety-Detail/ArtMID/3682/ArticleID/2841/FAARFIELD-20).
- **DRIP:** `drip-logo.png` is the cover wordmark and water artwork extracted from
  the embedded original cover image in FHWA's archived DRIP 2.0 user's guide.
  `drip-icon.png` is the application title-bar icon in Figure 1 of NCHRP Appendix
  TT. It is a native 16-pixel icon, displayed at small size.
  [FHWA manual](https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/pavement/pubs/010942.pdf),
  [NCHRP Appendix TT](https://content.aashtowarepavementme.org/Documents/1-37A/2appendices_TT.pdf).
- **Pavement ME:** `pavement-me-logo.png` renders the complete ME Design lockup
  from page 7 of the official 2026 brand guidelines. `pavement-me-icon.png` is the
  intact square mark from the same published lockup.
  [Brand guidelines](https://www.aashtoware.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/AASHTOWare_Brand_Guidelines_2026_v7.pdf),
  [official logo image](https://aashtowarepavementme.org/MEDesignWebAppIdp/images/pavement-me-design-logo-2026-01-23.png).
- **WinJULEA:** `winjulea-logo.png` is the historical software splash artwork
  reproduced in Figure 14.8 (printed page 217) of Instituto Mexicano del
  Transporte's Technical Document 72. `winjulea-icon.png` isolates the ERDC mark
  shown in that splash. These are published software branding, not a verified
  standalone WinJULEA product logo. The current PCASE 7 installer was inspected
  as an archive without executing it; it did not supply a WinJULEA asset.
  [IMT document](https://imt.mx/archivos/Publicaciones/DocumentoTecnico/dt72.pdf).

No installers, complete third-party manuals, or other software files are included
in this asset directory.
